Trust & transparency

How we verify
what we publish.

AUTOMATET is an international information and discovery platform. We aim to make crypto platform information easier to compare while clearly separating verified facts, unknowns and paid placements.

Platform directory

We use official provider websites and documentation to identify a platform, its broad service type and its published product information. Directory inclusion is not an endorsement, a guarantee of safety, or confirmation that a service is available in every country.

Trading fees

Fee observations are recorded with product scope, tier, official source URL and check date. Spot, futures, margin, special pairs, VIP levels, regional fee schedules and fee-token discounts are not interchangeable. If we cannot confidently identify the applicable scope and tier, we leave the rate blank rather than presenting a misleading universal number. Always confirm the live rate in your own account before trading.

Regional availability

We record availability by country using two-letter ISO country codes. A status of “available”, “restricted” or “unavailable” requires official evidence. “Unknown” means we have not confirmed the position; it does not mean the service is available. Laws, sanctions, licensing, product permissions and provider policies can change, so users must check the provider’s current terms for their own location.

Promotions and rewards

We only intend to publish a promotion after checking an official source, the offer terms, start and end dates, eligibility conditions, reward form and relevant geographic restrictions. Expired, unverifiable or unclear offers should not be promoted. A reward may be conditional, non-cash, locked or subject to trading requirements.

News and updates

The news desk checks enabled publisher RSS feeds on a rolling schedule and stores recent headlines for display. Initial sources include CoinDesk, Cointelegraph, The Block and Decrypt. We publish headlines with short excerpts and direct links to the original publisher; we do not reproduce full articles. Feed refreshes are attempted at least every 30 minutes, with a per-source cooldown to limit unnecessary requests. A publisher outage can delay an update. Summaries are written for clarity and are not a substitute for the full report. Publication dates and effective dates can differ; check the source for the exact scope and current status.

Advertising and affiliate relationships

Paid placements are labelled as sponsored. Some outbound links may be affiliate links, which can generate a commission for AUTOMATET at no additional cost to the user. Commercial relationships do not guarantee a positive review, a platform listing, or publication of a promotion. Editorial status and offer verification remain separate from sales discussions.

Corrections and contact

Platform fees, terms and regional access can change quickly. If you identify an outdated or incorrect item, contact us through the partner and contact page with the page URL and official supporting evidence. We may update, annotate or remove the item after review.

Important notice

AUTOMATET provides general information only, not financial, investment, legal or tax advice. Crypto assets and related services involve substantial risks, including loss of capital, smart-contract vulnerabilities, custody failures and regulatory restrictions. Make your own checks and seek qualified advice where appropriate.
