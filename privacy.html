Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains how AUTOMATET handles information submitted through its public website. It is an operational notice and should be reviewed for applicable legal requirements.

Information we may process

The public website is designed to display exchange profiles, verified promotions and links to third-party publishers. Browsing public pages does not require an account. If you use the Advertise / Partner inquiry form, we collect the company or project name, contact name, business email, optional website, inquiry category and message. Please do not include passwords, financial account details or sensitive personal information. We use these details to assess and respond to partnership inquiries. Inquiry records are stored in our database and are accessible to authorized AUTOMATET administrators.

Cookies and analytics

The partnership inquiry form does not require advertising cookies. Any future analytics, advertising pixels, or non-essential cookies will be assessed separately and disclosed with any required consent controls before activation.

Affiliate links and advertising

Some outbound links may be affiliate links. Sponsored content and paid placements will be clearly labelled. A partner may receive information when you visit its website; its own privacy policy governs that processing.

Retention and security

Inquiry information may be processed by the hosting and database providers needed to operate the site. We do not sell inquiry contact details. We retain inquiries for as long as reasonably needed to manage business discussions and operational records, then delete or anonymize them when no longer required. Administrative access is restricted and reasonable security controls are applied. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Third-party websites

Exchange, wallet, protocol and news publisher websites are operated independently. Check their terms, privacy policies, security practices and eligibility requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the AUTOMATET team through Telegram or X.

This notice is not legal advice. It should be reviewed against the privacy and data-protection rules that apply to AUTOMATET and its visitors.
