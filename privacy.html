Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This draft policy describes the intended privacy approach for AUTOMATET. It must be reviewed and updated before enabling analytics, advertising, affiliate tracking, contact forms or subscriptions.

Information we may process

The public website is designed to display exchange profiles, verified promotions and links to third-party publishers. We do not ask visitors to submit sensitive personal information to browse public pages. If you contact us through Telegram, X or another third-party service, that service's privacy policy applies to information you share there.

Cookies and analytics

We do not claim that analytics or advertising cookies are currently active. If we introduce analytics, advertising pixels, or non-essential cookies, this policy and any required consent controls will be updated before activation.

Affiliate links and advertising

Some outbound links may be affiliate links. Sponsored content and paid placements will be clearly labelled. A partner may receive information when you visit its website; its own privacy policy governs that processing.

Retention and security

We intend to collect only information needed to operate the service, restrict access to administrative systems, and apply reasonable retention and security controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Third-party websites

Exchange, wallet, protocol and news publisher websites are operated independently. Check their terms, privacy policies, security practices and eligibility requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the AUTOMATET team through Telegram or X.

This page is an operational draft, not legal advice. Replace it with a jurisdiction-reviewed policy before collecting personal data or activating ad technologies.
