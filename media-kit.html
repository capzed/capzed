Campaign measurement & transparency

Where tracking is enabled, reporting can include served impressions and outbound clicks recorded by AUTOMATET. These are delivery metrics, not guaranteed reach, registrations, deposits or sales. We do not publish fabricated audience figures or promise conversion results. Audience estimates and commercial forecasts are shared only when supported by measured analytics.

Who can partner?

Crypto exchanges, wallets, blockchain infrastructure, security tools, analytics products, education providers and other relevant brands.

What we need

Brand website, campaign objective, target countries, approved creative, destination URL, timing, budget range and official terms where applicable.

Editorial safeguards

Paid content is labelled. We review destinations and claims, distinguish sponsored campaigns from independently verified offers, and may reject misleading or unsuitable promotions.

Commercial packages and pricing are quoted individually based on placement, duration, targeting, creative requirements and available inventory. No fixed rate card or traffic guarantee is implied.
